# Business Questions

This project is structured as an analytics case study rather than a collection of disconnected SQL snippets.

1. What is the executive performance snapshot: revenue, orders, AOV, gross profit and margin?
2. How are revenue and active customers trending month over month?
3. Which geographic regions create the most commercial value?
4. What share of placed orders is cancelled or refunded?
5. Which customers should CRM and retention teams prioritize using RFM?
6. How does customer retention evolve by first-purchase cohort?
7. Which acquisition channels generate the highest-value and most repeat customers?
8. Which individual products drive revenue and gross profit?
9. Is revenue concentrated in a small share of the product catalog (Pareto analysis)?
10. Which product categories combine scale with healthy gross margin?

## SQL competencies demonstrated

- multi-table joins and aggregation
- conditional aggregation with `FILTER` and `CASE`
- CTEs and layered analytical logic
- `LAG`, `NTILE`, `DENSE_RANK`, `ROW_NUMBER` and cumulative window functions
- cohort construction and date arithmetic
- RFM segmentation
- Pareto / contribution analysis
- reusable views and metric definitions
- indexes and `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)`
- reconciliation and data-quality checks
